Models & Data

Find the right model for your research

Beetle provides an open collection of bilingual and monolingual language models, training checkpoints, datasets, and tokenisers for studying language learning and cross-lingual behaviour. Start with a research question, explore a model family, or search the full collection on Hugging Face.

285 bilingual models · 45 monolingual models · open research resources

What would you like to investigate?

Human language processing

Compare model predictions with human language behaviour. Find bilingual models and matched monolingual baselines for studying reading times, grammaticality judgements, and other measures of human language processing.

Cross-lingual transfer

Study how learning one language affects another. Compare language pairs, training scales, and exposure schedules to investigate when knowledge transfers across languages and how this depends on prior language experience.

Representation learning and interpretability

Investigate how linguistic knowledge develops inside models. Use model checkpoints and controlled training comparisons to study internal representations, capability development, and the mechanisms underlying cross-lingual behaviour.

New experiments

Build on Beetle to ask a different question. Combine released models, datasets, and tokenisers to develop new evaluations, compare learning strategies, or design follow-up experiments.

Collections

Choose a model family

Beetle's resources are organised into collections based on their training data and experimental purpose. Start with the collection that best matches your question.

Final release · recommended

Beetle-FineWeb-24B (final release)

Beetle-FineWeb-24B-4 · Beetle-FineWeb-24B-5 · 123 models

The final 24B models we are releasing. Bilingual models across all five exposure curricula (B1–B5) and monolingual baselines, trained on FineWeb at the largest scale. These two collections supersede the earlier experimental 24B variants and are the recommended checkpoints for new work.

24B-4 on HF 24B-5 on HF

Beetle-FineWeb-100M

FineWeb · 100M tokens

Small-scale experiments across many first languages. Monolingual controls and bilingual models trained on a 100-million-token FineWeb slice. A useful starting point for comparing first languages and exposure curricula under a relatively small training budget — study curriculum effects, compare language pairs, or run experiments feasible with modest compute.

Beetle-FineWeb-2B

FineWeb · 2B tokens

Intermediate-scale bilingual learning. Models trained on a 2-billion-token FineWeb slice, with monolingual baselines and multiple bilingual exposure schedules. Useful for investigating whether patterns observed at smaller scales persist as models receive more training.

Beetle-FineWeb2-24B

FineWeb2 · 24B tokens

Large-scale comparisons across bilingual curricula. Bilingual models trained on FineWeb2, with balanced, simultaneous, sequential, classroom, and late-exposure conditions across several language pairs. Selected variants also explore continual learning. Earlier 24B experiments — see the final release above for the recommended checkpoints.

Beetle-FineWeb3-24B

FineWeb3 · 24B tokens

Monolingual baselines across languages. A collection of monolingual models trained on FineWeb3, providing additional language-specific baselines for comparative analysis.

Beetle-HumanScale

Human-scale · models · data · tokenisers

Models for cognitively motivated experiments. Models, datasets, and tokenisers based on controlled, human-scale language-learning settings. Includes bilingual and monolingual models, alternative exposure schedules, and selected continual-learning variants — particularly useful for connecting computational experiments with questions about human language acquisition.

Beetle-Data

326 repositories · datasets · tokenisers · shards

The full data and tokeniser catalogue. BabyBabel corpora, bilingual datasets, BPE tokenisers, pre-tokenised shards and raw corpora used across the Beetle experiments. Everything you need to build new data pipelines or reproduce training conditions.

Data & tokenisers

Datasets, corpora and tokenisers

Repository names encode most of this information, so the finder above (set Resource to Datasets, Tokenisers, Pre-tokenised or Raw corpora) will take you straight to any of these.

BabyBabel corpora

Small, language-specific corpora designed for controlled language-model training. Available in multiple languages and data sizes, with held-out datasets in the HumanScale collection.

Bilingual datasets

Parallel experimental resources for training models on different language combinations and exposure conditions. Use these to construct new comparisons or investigate language learning beyond the released checkpoints.

Tokenisers

Bilingual and monolingual BPE tokenisers developed for the Beetle experiments. Shared tokenisation choices support comparisons between training conditions; HumanScale also provides tokenisers for additional language combinations.

Pre-tokenised & raw corpora

Pre-tokenised training shards for supported configurations, alongside larger raw corpora for researchers who want to build their own data pipelines.

Next steps

Need help choosing a checkpoint?

If you are unsure which models or datasets suit your experiment, start with the framework to understand the training conditions, or consult the documentation for instructions on using the resources. For the full scientific motivation and evaluation details, read the EMNLP 2026 paper.

Have a new research question? Beetle is an open resource for collaborative research. We welcome projects using the released models to investigate bilingualism, cross-lingual transfer, representation learning, and other questions about how language models learn.
