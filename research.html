For everyone

What can YOU do with Beetle?

Whether you are interested in building small language models, AI interpretability, building better AI systems for more languages, or researching how humans learn language, the Beetle model suite and our open-source training, data and analysis tools let you ask cool questions.

For AI enthusiasts, researchers & model builders

What you can do with Beetle
  • AI enthusiasts. Get hands-on with open models and explore questions that go beyond benchmark scores.
  • AI researchers. Investigate model internals: tokenisation, memorisation, representations and interpretability. Learn when capabilities emerge during training and test hypotheses about learning dynamics, exploring what changes as models see more data (100M, 2B and 24B training tokens).
  • Multilingual NLP researchers. A suite of monolingual and bilingual models with checkpoints, from Finnish and Basque to Hindi, Japanese and Korean, for studying how multilingual transfer happens.
  • Model builders. Train your own small language models, experiment with different data mixtures and language-exposure curricula, and see what happens when you change how a model learns.

Beetle isn't just for AI engineers

What you can do with Beetle
  • Compare AI with human learning. Compare the behaviour of language models with human cognitive data.
  • Learn the science behind AI models. Use Beetle for hands-on exploration of the science behind language models: analyse our models and learn how to train your own.

Explore the models in Colab, no coding required →

With 330 monolingual and bilingual models, spanning 21 first languages, five language-exposure curricula, and approximately 30 checkpoints per model, Beetle gives researchers an unprecedentedly controlled way to investigate how models acquire and process multiple languages. Our open-source Beetle Framework brings together open model weights, training checkpoints, data, tokenisers and analysis tools in one research suite.
